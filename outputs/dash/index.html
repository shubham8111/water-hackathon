How much inflow 1 mm of rain produces, by delay

Extra KRS inflow (cusecs) from 1 mm of rain falling k days earlier on wet soil, by distance band. Response peaks after about 2 days and lasts about a week. Dashed: the same rain on dry soil.

How upstream releases reach KRS, by delay

Cusecs arriving at KRS per cusec released upstream k days earlier. Most of the water arrives within 0–2 days.

What the model relies on

Drop in test NSE when each group of inputs is shuffled. Soil wetness (rain over past weeks) matters most, ahead of any single day's rain.

How early can engineers know? Forecast error stays near the long-term-average baseline (— TMC) through July. It falls to — TMC by 15 Aug and — TMC by 15 Sep. A reasonably informed estimate is possible from mid-August. Before that, the outcome depends on rain that has not fallen yet.

Forecast error by forecast date

RMSE of the 30-Sep storage forecast, in TMC, by leave-one-year-out cross-validation over 1991–2025. Lower is better. Each model is refit for each forecast date.
